## About Tech Innovators Inc.

Tech Innovators Inc. is a global technology company with a mission to build innovative solutions that empower people and organizations worldwide. Founded in 2010, we've grown to serve millions of users across six continents while maintaining our commitment to ethical technology and inclusive practices.

Our products and services span cloud infrastructure, developer tools, and enterprise software solutions. We believe that the best technology is built by diverse teams bringing different perspectives and experiences to the table.

## Our Commitment to Open Source

Open source is at the heart of everything we do. Over 80% of our technology stack is built on open source foundations, and we actively contribute back to the communities that make our work possible. We maintain several popular open source projects and employ dozens of maintainers who dedicate their time to advancing open source technology.

## Why We Support Women of Open Source

The technology industry has historically struggled with diversity and inclusion, particularly in open source communities. Women and non-binary individuals remain significantly underrepresented as both contributors and maintainers.

We partner with Women of Open Source because we believe that diverse teams build better software. By supporting this community, we're helping to:

- **Remove barriers to entry** for women and underrepresented groups in open source
- **Amplify voices** that have traditionally been marginalized in tech spaces
- **Create opportunities** for mentorship, speaking engagements, and career advancement
- **Build networks** that connect talented individuals with meaningful projects and employment

## Our Partnership Activities

As a partner, we actively support Women of Open Source through:

- **Sponsorship** of community events, workshops, and conferences
- **Mentorship programs** connecting our engineers with community members
- **Speaking opportunities** at our internal and external tech events
- **Hiring initiatives** actively seeking diverse talent from the community
- **Open source contributions** encouraging our team to contribute to community projects
