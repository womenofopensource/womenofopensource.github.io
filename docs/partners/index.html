Become a Partner

Join us in supporting women and underrepresented groups in open source! As a partner of the Women of Open Source community, you'll be helping to create a more inclusive and diverse technology ecosystem.

  • Opportunities to connect with talented contributors and maintainers

  • Support events, workshops, and initiatives that make a difference
