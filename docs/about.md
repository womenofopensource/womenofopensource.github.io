## Our mission

The Women of Open Source community is a global community dedicated to supporting, celebrating, and amplifying the voices of women who contribute to open source software. We provide a welcoming space for both cis and trans women and non-binary and gender-nonconforming people who are interested in and/or contributing to Open Source. We welcome folk at all stages of their open source journey - from those taking their first steps to experienced maintainers and leaders.

## What we do

### Community and connection

We bring together women from around the world who are passionate about open source. Through our online platforms and in-person events, we create opportunities for networking, collaboration, and friendship.

### Mentorship and learning

Whether you're making your first contribution or leading a major project, we are developing mentorship programs, workshops, and resources to help you grow your skills and confidence in the open source ecosystem.

### Visibility and opportunities

We maintain a directory of speakers and showcase the incredible work being done by women in open source. Our platform helps connect our community members with speaking opportunities and career advancement.

### Advocacy and support

We advocate for inclusive practices in open source communities and provide support for women facing challenges. We believe that diverse perspectives make open source stronger and more innovative.

## Who we serve

Our community welcomes:

- **Contributors**: Women actively contributing to open source projects
- **Maintainers**: Women leading and maintaining open source projects
- **Aspiring Contributors**: Women looking to start their open source journey
- **Allies**: Anyone committed to supporting women in open source

## Get involved

There are many ways to participate in our community:

- Join our online discussions and networking events
- Apply to be listed in our speakers directory by making a PR to our repo (you'll need to be an active member of our community first)
- Attend conferences and meetups where we're present
- Contribute to our community resources and documentation
- [Partner](/partners/) with us to support women in open source

## Our values

**Inclusivity**: We welcome both cis and trans women and non-binary and gender-nonconforming people from all backgrounds, experience levels, and technical disciplines.

**Empowerment**: We believe in lifting each other up and creating opportunities for growth.

**Collaboration**: We foster a spirit of cooperation and mutual support.

**Excellence**: We celebrate technical achievement and encourage continuous learning.

**Authenticity**: We create safe spaces where women can be themselves and share their experiences.
