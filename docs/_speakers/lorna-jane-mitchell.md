Lorna is an API Architect at TM Forum, shaping industry standards and turning them into real-world solutions worldwide. Based in Yorkshire, UK, she is a recognised expert in open standards and developer experience, and a published author. Lorna serves on the OpenAPI Technical Steering Committee, helping to guide and grow this widely used API standard, sits on the OpenUK board, and maintains open source software projects. Keep up with her work at [lornajane.net](https://lornajane.net).
