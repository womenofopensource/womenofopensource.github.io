Sīlavāpi (ex Ruth) Cheesley is a passionate advocate for open source who has dedicated her career to building and nurturing vibrant communities. As the Project Lead for Mautic, the world's largest open source marketing automation platform, she works to ensure the project remains accessible, sustainable, and community-driven.

With extensive experience in community management, governance, and open source project leadership, Sīlavāpi is committed to helping projects create inclusive environments where contributors of all backgrounds and skill levels can thrive. She is particularly passionate about documentation, mentorship, and creating pathways for new contributors.

Sīlavāpi regularly speaks at conferences worldwide on topics including community building, open source governance, and sustainable contribution models. She is known for her practical, down-to-earth approach and her commitment to putting community first.
