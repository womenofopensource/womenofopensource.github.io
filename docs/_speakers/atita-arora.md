Atita Arora is a hands-on search and AI engineer whose work runs from enterprise search and NLP to vector search, RAG, and agentic systems. She has built and scaled search at Idealo and MYTOYS Group, and worked as a Solutions Architect at Qdrant.

In open source, Atita is a PMC member of [Apache OpenNLP](https://opennlp.apache.org/) and serves on the program committee for Berlin Buzzwords. She co-leads Women of Search, a community for women working in AI, search, and agents, where she runs events and helps new speakers get their first stage.

She is a regular speaker at Haystack and Berlin Buzzwords, and writes on VectorHub, including a three-part series on RAG evaluation. Her current focus: why the search community's decades of relevance expertise are exactly what today's AI agents and their memory systems are missing.

You can also hear her on the [Vector Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BVM6TUSfn3E) talking vector search for e-commerce. More slides are on [Speaker Deck](https://speakerdeck.com/atarora).

Atita is based in Berlin.
