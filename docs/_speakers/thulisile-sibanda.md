Thulisile is an Open Source maintainer and community builder who works across documentation, programs, governance, and contributor experience. She spends her time building, scaling, and sustaining global developer communities, by improving how Open Source projects are run, how knowledge is shared, and how communities grow sustainably.

Currently, Thulie is involved in the AsyncAPI Initiative, where she contributes both strategically and hands-on as Chair of the Governance Board and a member of the Technical Steering Committee. She helps guide direction, support working groups, mentor contributors, and stay close to the day-to-day realities of the community.

In practice, her work looks like:
- Designing and running Open Source programs and working groups
- Maintaining community processes and governance practices
- Supporting contributors and helping them grow into long-term participation and leadership
- Organizing community conferences and events
- Writing and improving developer documentation and educational content
- Using data, feedback, and observation to improve how communities operate

Thulie cares deeply about Open Source, inclusive communities, and building spaces where contributors can learn, grow, and feel supported.
