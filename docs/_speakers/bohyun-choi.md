Bohyun Choi is a platform engineer focused on Kubernetes, GPU infrastructure, and AI platforms. She builds and operates production systems for GPU and AI workloads, with a particular interest in reliability, observability, and making complex infrastructure easier to operate.

She is a Kubestronaut and the creator and maintainer of KUBE-RCA, an open-source project exploring how LLM agents can support Kubernetes incident investigation using live cluster evidence rather than relying on generated answers alone. She presented the project at Open Source Summit North America 2026.

Bohyun enjoys sharing practical lessons from operating cloud-native and AI infrastructure and contributing to open-source communities. Her speaking interests include Kubernetes, platform engineering, GPU and AI infrastructure, SRE, observability, and the practical use of AI agents in infrastructure operations.
