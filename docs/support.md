Women of Open Source is run by volunteers, and we keep the community free to join and take part in. Some things still cost money though, and that is where you can help.

We use **Open Collective** to accept and manage contributions. It is fully transparent, so you can see exactly what comes in and where every penny goes.

<a class="button button--large" href="{{ site.data.settings.support_settings.open_collective_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Support us on Open Collective</a>

## Where your contribution goes

Your support helps us cover the practical costs of running and growing the community, including:

- Getting our members to conferences and events, where they can speak, learn and connect
- Tools and services that keep the community and this website running
- Mentorship programmes, workshops and resources as we build them out
- Making our events and spaces as welcoming and accessible as we can

## Other ways to help

Not in a position to contribute financially? That is completely fine, and there are lots of other ways to get involved:

- [Partner]({{ '/partners/' | relative_url }}) with us if you represent an organisation that wants to support women in open source
- [Join the community]({{ '/application/' | relative_url }}) and take part
- Share our work with people who would value it

Thank you for helping us support women in open source. 💜
